# Shameer SN

Personal brand for Shameer SN, a senior software architect (14+ years) who builds and rescues full-stack JavaScript, AWS and AI products, mostly in healthcare and edtech. Used for the website (shameer.sn), LinkedIn banner, proposals, invoices and slide decks.

## The idea

**Engineered, calm, trustworthy.** The buyer is a founder or product lead who needs someone senior to own the architecture. The brand should feel like a clear system diagram, not a flashy agency.

The mark is an **S drawn as a routed connection**: one wire turning at right angles, with a node at each end. It says "I connect systems" without saying it. The accent colour, **laterite**, is the red-brown of Kerala laterite stone: a quiet signal of where Shameer works from.

## Content fundamentals (voice)

- Short, plain sentences. One idea per sentence. English that a non-native reader understands on first read.
- Say what was done and what changed. "Moved the API from PHP to Node.js. Cut unused endpoints by a third." Not "leveraged cutting-edge synergies".
- First person, singular: "I". Use "we" only when a team delivered it.
- Numbers beat adjectives. If there is no number yet, write the fact plainly. Never invent a figure.
- Banned words: leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, passionate, ninja, rockstar, seamless, robust (unless technical), "in today's fast-paced world".
- Headlines: say the outcome for the client. Example: "Senior architecture for health and learning products, without hiring a CTO."

## Visual foundations

- **Ground:** `paper` (warm off-white). Alternate sections with `surface`. Use `night` for one strong band per page (CTA or footer).
- **Accent:** `laterite` only for the primary action, links and one key number per view. Never as a big background except the CTA button.
- **Secondary:** `backwater` for domain tags (Healthcare, EdTech), check marks and diagram nodes.
- **Lines, not shadows.** 1px `line` borders on cards. No drop shadows, no gradients, no glassmorphism.
- **Type:** Schibsted Grotesk for everything readable. JetBrains Mono, uppercase `label` style, for small meta labels ("CASE STUDY", "AWS · NODE.JS"), section numbers and code. Display sizes are tight (-0.02em).
- **Grid:** 12 columns, 1200px max content width, 24px gutters (`space-3`), 96px between sections (`space-5`).
- **Radius:** `radius-md` (8px) for buttons and cards. Pill shape only for the "Available for projects" badge.
- **Imagery:** one real, natural photo of Shameer (not a studio stock look). Architecture diagrams drawn in the brand: `ink` lines, `backwater` nodes, one `laterite` path for the "important flow". No stock photos of people at laptops, no AI-generated people.

## Iconography

Simple 1.5px stroke line icons (Lucide style), `ink` or `muted`. Never emoji. Icons support labels; they never replace them.

## Logo usage

- `sn-mark.svg` (dark tile) on paper or surface. `sn-mark-light.svg` (light tile) on `night`.
- Wordmark = the mark + "Shameer SN" set in Schibsted Grotesk 600, ink, 8px gap per 32px of mark height.
- Minimum mark size 24px. Keep clear space = one node diameter around it.
- Do not recolour the route, rotate it, or add effects.

## Components (for the website)

- **Button primary:** `laterite` fill, `on-laterite` text, 8px radius, 48px tall, 20px side padding.
- **Button secondary:** transparent, 1px `ink` border, `ink` text.
- **Tag:** mono label, `surface` fill, `backwater` or `muted` text, 4px radius.
- **Case-study card:** `surface` fill, 1px `line` border, mono label on top, h3 title, one-line result, tags at the bottom.
- **Stat:** large number in `laterite` (h1 size), muted caption under it. Only real, checked numbers.
