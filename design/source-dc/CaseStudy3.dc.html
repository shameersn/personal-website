CASE STUDY · HEALTHCARE · 2020–2023

A notification system for a healthcare app

I designed and built how a healthcare app sends its emails and handles audio and video calls. It runs on AWS managed services, so there are no message servers to look after.

CLIENT
Healthcare app
MY ROLE
Architect and developer
STACK
Node.js, React, PostgreSQL
CLOUD
AWS SNS, SQS, Lambda, SES · Twilio

01 · THE PROBLEM

Messages had to be reliable and easy to scale

A healthcare app sends a lot of messages, and people depend on them. The design had to handle busy times without losing messages, and the app also needed audio and video calls.

02 · WHAT I DID

Events, queues and small workers

  • The app publishes events to Amazon SNS.
  • SQS queues hold the work, so nothing is lost when traffic jumps.
  • Small Lambda functions process the queues and send email through Amazon SES.
  • Twilio powers the audio and video calls.
  • Node.js and React for the app, PostgreSQL for the data.

03 · THE RESULT

Scales with the load, no servers to manage

Serverless

workers scale up and down with the queue

Queued

messages wait safely when traffic is high

Email + calls

one design for messages, audio and video
